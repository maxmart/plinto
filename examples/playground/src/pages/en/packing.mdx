<Prose id="pros-1f6b3d9c"
  heading="The list"
>
  The shop sells rope, milk and stamps, in that order of reliability. Everything else comes over on the ferry, which means everything else is your responsibility.

  - A wind layer you already trust
  - Shoes that accept being wet as a lifestyle
  - A refillable water bottle — the wells are good where marked
  - Cash, in coins, for the honesty boxes
  - One book more than you think you need

  What you can leave behind: a schedule, strong opinions about coffee, and anything that needs charging more than once a day.
</Prose>

<Callout id="call-4e8a2c7f"
  tone="info"
>
  **Winter crossings.** Between November and March the ferry runs a reduced table and the sea runs an expanded one. Pack a headlamp, double your margins, and treat the last boat of the day as a rumour rather than a promise.
</Callout>
