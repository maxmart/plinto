<Prose id="pros-8b1d4f6a"
  heading="Why small islands?"
>
  Big islands have airports, timetables and opinions of their own. Small islands have a ferry that leaves when the skipper is ready, a noticeboard that is the closest thing to a newspaper, and weather that decides your plans for you. This guide is about the second kind.

  We have been keeping these notes for years, first in a tin box on the boat and now here. They are opinionated, occasionally out of date, and written from the quay rather than from an office. When a note disagrees with the sea, trust the sea.

  Start with the islands themselves, then read up on how to visit without annoying anyone — the two pages below are the short course.
</Prose>
