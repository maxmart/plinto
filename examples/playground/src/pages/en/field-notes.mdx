  The rule of thumb from the Åsvik pilots: anything \< 10 °C wants a wetsuit, anything \< 4 °C wants a different plan. The öre-sized jellyfish of late Küstensommer are harmless; the größe of the swell is not.

  Before any swim, in order:

  1. Check the tide table below
     - the painted one on the boathouse wall is older but not wronger
  2. Check the flag on the harbour mast
     1. green means the pilots approve
     2. no flag means nobody has looked, which is not the same thing
  3. Tell someone with a kettle
     - they will have it on by the time you are back

  The tide clock on the noticeboard runs on this, verbatim — do not translate the code, the sea does not speak German either:

  ```python
  def next_high(now, station):
      # lunar day: 24 h 50 min, halved for the semi-diurnal tide
      period = (24 * 60 + 50) / 2
      return station.last_high + timedelta(minutes=period)
  ```
