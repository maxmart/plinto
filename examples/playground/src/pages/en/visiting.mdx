<Prose id="pros-9a4d7f2b"
  heading="When to go"
>
  June is polite, July is crowded, August is the local secret. September is better than all three if you can read a forecast, and the shoulder months belong to the birds and to the people who fix things.

  Whenever you go, tell the harbour office when you arrive. Not because anyone checks — because when the fog comes in, the list of who is on the island is the only list there is.
</Prose>
