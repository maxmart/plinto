<Prose id="pros-6d2f8b4a"
  heading="About the guide"
>
  Driftwood is written by the people who run the mail boat, plus whoever happens to be aboard with an opinion. Nobody is paid, nothing is sponsored, and no island has ever asked to be included — which several of them consider a point of pride.

  The guide exists in three languages, and the three versions are edited by different hands. They drift apart and get pulled back together; that is normal here, not an accident.
</Prose>
