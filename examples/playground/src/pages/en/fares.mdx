  These are the numbers painted on the harbour wall, reproduced exactly. They change once a year, by vote, at the meeting everyone claims not to attend.

  | Route | Single | Return | Bicycle |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Mainland – Aldra | 60 kr | 100 kr | 20 kr |
  | Aldra – Lågskär | 40 kr | 70 kr | 15 kr |
  | Mainland – Stenholm | 90 kr | 150 kr | 25 kr |

  Children under twelve travel free, as does anything that fits in a pocket and stays there.
</Prose>
